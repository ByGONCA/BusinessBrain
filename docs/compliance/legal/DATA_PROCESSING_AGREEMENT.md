# BusinessBrain Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

> **Document Status**: Template Draft  
> **Regulatory Basis**: Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)  
> **Applicability**: Governs the processing of Customer Personal Data within BusinessBrain workspaces.  
> `[LEGAL_COUNSEL_REQUIRED: Formal legal counsel validation before enterprise execution]`

---

## 1. Scope & Relationship of Parties

1. **Definitions**:
   - &quot;Customer&quot; means the corporate organization subscribing to BusinessBrain.
   - &quot;Company&quot; means BusinessBrain (`[OWNER_INPUT_REQUIRED: Legal Entity Name]`).
   - &quot;Customer Personal Data&quot; means personal data processed by Company on behalf of Customer in the provision of the Service.
2. **Roles of Parties**: The parties agree that Customer is the **Data Controller** (or a Processor acting on behalf of a third-party Controller), and Company is the **Data Processor** under GDPR Article 28.

---

## 2. Processor Obligations (GDPR Article 28(3))

Company agrees to:
1. **Documented Instructions**: Process Customer Personal Data strictly on documented instructions from Customer, including regarding transfers outside the EEA.
2. **Confidentiality**: Ensure that persons authorized to process Customer Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality.
3. **Security Measures (Article 32)**: Implement and maintain robust technical and organizational measures (TOMs), including encryption at rest (AES-256), encryption in transit (TLS 1.3), database-level Row-Level Security, and automated rate limiting.
4. **Subprocessor Engagement**: Customer grants general authorization to engage the subprocessors listed in the [Subprocessor Inventory](../DATA_PROCESSING_MAP.md). Company will notify Customer of any planned changes with reasonable notice.
5. **Assistance with Data Subject Rights**: Provide technological tools (including JSON data export and account erasure functions) allowing Customer to fulfill data subject requests under GDPR Chapter III.
6. **Data Breach Notification**: Notify Customer without undue delay and within **72 hours** of becoming aware of a confirmed personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data.
7. **Deletion or Return**: Upon termination of the Service, delete or return all Customer Personal Data at Customer's election, except where EU or Member State law requires statutory retention.
8. **Audits & Verification**: Make available to Customer information necessary to demonstrate compliance with these obligations.

---

## 3. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Where Customer Personal Data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA), the parties shall ensure adequate protection through:
- European Commission Adequacy Decisions, or
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.
